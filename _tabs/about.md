delivering cutting-edge solutions for the **Banking, Financial Services &
Insurance (BFSI)** domain.

With **17+ years in the software industry** — across both **product-based** and

## What I do

As a Forward Deployed Architect, I work **close to the customer** — sitting
between engineering and the business to make sure the solution actually fits the
problem. Day to day, that means:

- **Solution & enterprise architecture** — translating business goals into
  designs that are secure, resilient, and built to scale in regulated BFSI
  environments.
- **Cutting-edge delivery** — bringing emerging technology and modern
  engineering practices to real-world financial-services challenges.
- **Hands-on partnership** — engaging directly with stakeholders to shape,
  prototype, and deploy solutions, then iterating as their needs evolve.
- **Bridging product and services thinking** — drawing on years of building
  products and delivering client engagements to balance reusability with a
  tailored fit.
